Questions

Does HushScan work offline?

Yes. Scanning, text recognition (OCR), file conversion and every editing tool run on your iPhone. Your documents are never uploaded to a server.

Does HushScan add a watermark to scans?

No. Scans and exported PDFs have no watermark, on the free plan too. A watermark tool is there only if you want to add your own.

Do I need an account?

No. HushScan has no sign-in. Open the app and scan.

Is HushScan free?

Scanning, PDF export and text recognition are free. HushScan Pro unlocks every tool without limits and removes ads.

Can I convert a scan to Word or Excel?

Yes. HushScan turns scans into editable Word, Excel and PowerPoint files on your iPhone.
