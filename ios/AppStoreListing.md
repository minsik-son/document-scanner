**Description:**

HushScan turns paper into clean PDFs and editable files — right on your iPhone. No account. No upload. No watermark on your scans.

SCAN
• Auto edge detection, perspective fix and clean white pages
• Multi-page PDFs, ID cards (both sides on one page), receipts, whiteboards and books
• Text recognition (OCR) so you can search and copy text from every scan

TURN IT INTO
• Word, Excel and PowerPoint you can edit
• Photo translation that keeps the original layout (iOS 26)
• Math problems solved step by step, long images, image sets

FIX AND SEND
• Hide personal info, fill forms, sign and annotate
• Compress large PDFs, add a password, merge, split and reorder pages
• Watermark and timestamp tools when you want them

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Every tool runs on your iPhone. HushScan has no server that receives your documents, no sign-in and no tracking. Add Face ID app lock and keep everything to yourself.

FREE AND PRO
Scanning, PDF export and text recognition are free. Try each Pro tool for free first. HushScan Pro unlocks every tool without limits and removes ads.
• Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. Manage or cancel in your Apple Account settings.
• A one-time Lifetime purchase is also available.
